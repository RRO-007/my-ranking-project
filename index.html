📖 FIFA SUM Calculation Methodology (Wikipedia Specs)

Since August 2018, FIFA calculates ratings using an adapted Elo algorithm known as the SUM formula after every match.

Formula: P = P_before + I * (W - W_e) Variables & Dropdown Choices: 1. P_before : Team points prior to the match. 2. I (Importance Coefficient): • 5 : Friendly outside International Match Calendar window • 10 : Friendly inside International Match Calendar window • 15 : Nations League (group stage) • 25 : Nations League play-offs/finals, Continental/World Cup qualifiers • 35 : Confederation final competition matches (before quarter-finals) • 40 : Confederation final competition matches (quarter-finals onwards) • 50 : FIFA World Cup matches (before quarter-finals) • 60 : FIFA World Cup matches (quarter-finals onwards) 3. W (Match Result): • 1.0 : Win in regular or extra time • 0.75 : Win in penalty shoot-out (PSO) • 0.5 : Draw OR Loss in penalty shoot-out (PSO) • 0.0 : Loss in regular or extra time 4. W_e (Expected Outcome): W_e = 1 / (10^(-Δ / 600) + 1) where Δ = P_before(Team A) - P_before(Team B) Condition Exceptions: • Knockout Protection : In knockout stages of final competitions, if ΔP < 0 for losing team or PSO winner, ΔP is forced to 0 (P cannot decrease). • Two-Legged Tie PSO : If a tie winner is decided on PSO in 2nd leg, the PSO is disregarded and match is evaluated as regular game.
